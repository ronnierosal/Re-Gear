# Getting Started with Re-Gear

Re-Gear adds console-style controls and automation to SteamOS handhelds. The goal is simple: spend less time managing the handheld and more time playing.

## Where Re-Gear lives

Re-Gear runs as a Decky plugin and is designed around controller-friendly navigation.

## Your first stop: Command Center

The Command Center brings together the controls and status you are most likely to need while playing.

You will see prominent everyday controls such as volume and brightness, plus your customizable Quick Access actions.

[Meet the Command Center →](command-center.md)

## Deeper settings

Use Re-Gear's other sections when you want to configure a feature rather than trigger a quick action:

- **Performance** — performance and display-related controls
- **eGPU** — external GPU and docking behavior
- **Controllers** — controller-related status and options
- **Settings** — preferences and customization

## Controller basics

Re-Gear is intended to be usable without reaching for a keyboard or mouse. Use the D-pad/stick to move, **A** to select, and **B** to go back. Where supported, **LB/RB** moves between top-level sections.
