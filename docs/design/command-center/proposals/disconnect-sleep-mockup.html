Notes

Refined set: what changed

  • A dark circle and faint circuit lines sit behind each icon, as on the sheet.
  • #10: braided cable, shiny USB-C plug with the USB symbol, silver tip, glowing cyan arrow.
  • #11: thin blue crescent with a soft blue glow, instead of the oversized flat white moon.
  • #12: glowing red power symbol, fully inside the tile, instead of a heavy filled disc cut off at the edge.
  • Same background, border and 240×144 size, and no text in the artwork.

Problems with the current tile

  • A moon on its own reads as Sleep. The eGPU tab also has a separate Sleep action that keeps the eGPU connected, so users can confuse the two.
  • The disconnect and unplug step, which is the part that matters for safety, isn't shown at all.
  • Flat white and purple shapes with no rim or metal. It looks lighter than the plug (#10) and ringed disc (#12) beside it.

A: plug + moon badge

  • Uses the Safe Disconnect plug at 80% size, so all three disconnect tiles look like one family.
  • The moon moves to a badge, the same idea as the check badge on #26 Game Ready.
  • Reads in order: unplug first, then sleep. At small sizes it's the busier of the two.

B: moon disc + unplug badge

  • Uses the same disc as #12 Shutdown, so Sleep and Shutdown look like a pair (violet and red).
  • The cyan unplug badge adds the disconnect meaning without crowding the moon.
  • The crescent has a gradient and rim glow to match the depth of the tiles next to it.

Copy nit (separate from the art)

  • The labels don't match: Disconnect + Sleep vs Safe Disconnect + Shutdown (egpu-action-panel.tsx). Suggest using Disconnect + Shutdown, which already matches the registry shortLabel.
